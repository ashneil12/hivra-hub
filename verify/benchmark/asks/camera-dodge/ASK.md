# What Ash asked for

> sometimes I'm dodging but my keys get reversed, pressing W but it goes backwards

The bug shows up once the camera has been turned: with the camera round behind the player, W dodges straight at it.
Dodging is supposed to go where the key points on the screen, whichever way the camera is facing — W away from the
camera, S toward it, A and D left and right of it.

`node test.mjs` is the project's own check and has to keep passing.
