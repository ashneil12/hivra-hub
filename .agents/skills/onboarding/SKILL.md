# Onboarding without a tour

Ash does not want a feature walk-through. He wants to know, in a few lines, where the project stands, what needs
him, and what to do next. Everything here comes from what is already true, not from a pitch.

## 1. Read before you speak
- Project memory first (`~/agent-memory/<project>/`): decisions, never-rules, what broke, lessons. Latest wins.
- The project's asks and open work, then the repo's `AGENTS.md`/`CLAUDE.md` if it has one.
- Look at what the hub itself already shows: Today, Needs you, Being built, the project's tasks.
- Never ask Ash something the memory, the code or the hub already answers.

## 2. Say it in three lines
1. **Where it stands:** what this project is doing right now, in his words, no jargon.
2. **What needs him:** only the things that genuinely wait on his decision or his tap. If nothing does, say that.
3. **The first move:** one action you can take now, or one thing for him to tap. One, not a list.

If he has a screen in front of him, name the place in the app ("Pages → Agents → Add") rather than describing the
feature.

## 3. Give him a page to look at, once
The hub ships ready-made pages (Pages → Ready-made): **Agents**, **Needs you**, **Being built**, **Projects**.
Add one, look at its preview, then **Keep and allow** if it answers a question he actually has. Do not keep all four
for him, and do not build a fifth page that repeats one of them: a real page he keeps beats a demonstration.

## 4. Rules that keep onboarding honest
- Real data only. An empty section says "Nothing here yet"; it is never filled with example rows.
- A page reads only what Ash allows. Show him the asks in plain words before he keeps it, and never ask for a
  permission the page does not use.
- A page has no internet of its own and can never hold a key. If a source is needed, Ash connects it on the page
  (Web sources → Connect); the app keeps the token, the page never sees it.
- Nothing is kept, allowed or sent by itself. He taps Keep and allow; you do the rest only after that.
- Do not open with the hub's architecture. Answer the question he asked, then stop.

## 5. Before you hand back
- One line on what you changed (if anything) and what you verified. Say plainly what you could not check.
- Leave the next step with him only when it truly needs him; otherwise take it and report back.
