The first mate runs this phase.

Sharpen a plan by interview. Ask one question at a time, about a choice the plan leaves open. Wait for the answer before the next question. Stop when the plan says what will be built, what will not, and how someone can tell it worked.

Do not turn the interview into a build. Write the answers into the plan document that already exists.
